# Executive Summary

This project explores the Airbnb listings market in New York City using a structured analytics workflow that includes data understanding, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, insight generation, and dashboard preparation.

The analysis found that listings are heavily concentrated in Manhattan and Brooklyn, and that entire homes/apartments and private rooms dominate the market. Review activity and annual availability also vary across boroughs.

A key contribution of this project is its data quality validation approach. During the analysis, several anomalies were identified, especially in price-related variables. As a result, the project distinguishes between high-confidence findings and cautionary findings.

This makes the project more transparent, analytically credible, and professionally relevant for a portfolio context.
