# Business Context

Short-term rental data can help describe how accommodation supply is distributed across a city and how listing activity differs by location and property type.

In this project, New York City was used as the market context because of its size, density, and diversity across boroughs. This makes it a useful setting for an exploratory descriptive analysis.

The project does not aim to build predictive models. Instead, its purpose is to produce a structured, professional, and transparent analytical case study suitable for a portfolio.
